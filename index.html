Salah Ghoubal's project

3D architectural project

Good morning everyone. My name is Salah Ghoubal. I am a 6th-year STE student at Lycée Mehdi Ben Barka in Oujda. Today, I am very happy to present my 3D architectural project. It is a modern small house designed for a small family. The house includes two comfortable bedrooms, one bathroom, a kitchen, and a living room. The design is simple, clean, and functional, with large windows to let in natural light and a nice garden area around the house.

I got the idea and inspiration for this design from modern foreign architecture because I really liked the style. I created this entire 3D model by myself using the software SketchUp. My main goal for this project is not just to pass my school year, but to learn a new, different skill that I can use and benefit from in the future. I am very interested in 3D design and architecture. Thank you for listening, and I am ready for your questions.
